# Active rebrand shortlist — 2026-09-01

This is the current active shortlist. Earlier candidates are research history, not active recommendations.

## Retained by decision

1. **Indexiva** — strong sound and clear index association; known caveat: close search/brand neighbours Indexive and Indexia.
2. **Indexmetry** — direct index + measurement meaning; cleaner exact SERPs, but a more technical and less fluid pronunciation.

## Nine new candidates

1. **Graphmetry** — graph + measurement; broad enough for rankings, links, competitors, clusters and technical site structure.
2. **CrawlFoundry** — a coherent platform metaphor: raw crawl/search data is turned into decisions and workflows.
3. **SignalMetra** — search signals + measurement; broad fit for an SEO suite beyond crawling alone.
4. **Crawlmetry** — compact crawl + measurement construction; exact searches returned no established exact-name brand in the checked DE/US SERPs. A small GitHub project named `clawmetry` is the nearest visible neighbour.
5. **LexiMetra** — lexicon/language + measurement; a strong fit for keywords, queries and content intelligence.
6. **Searcharium** — a memorable all-in-one "place for search"; broad product fit and intentionally more playful than a literal tool name.
7. **CrawlCommand** — operational and on-demand; communicates control over crawl and research workflows.
8. **Crawlvera** — crawl + `vera` (true); distinctive, though Google sometimes tests a correction toward Crawl4AI.
9. **CrawlDrive** — short, active and understandable; communicates an execution engine more than an analytics database.

## Current evidence boundary

- Verisign RDAP returned `404 / no record` for all eleven `.com` names at the final check on 2026-09-01. This is a point-in-time availability signal, not a reservation.
- Both fuzzy and quoted-exact Google organic SERPs were checked in Germany (`2276/de`) and the United States (`2840/en`) through DataForSEO.
- Candidates with obvious active brands, strong phonetic corrections or same-category neighbours were removed from the active list.
- Trademark, company-register, social-handle and app-store clearance are not yet proven. Those checks belong after the shortlist is reduced to roughly three names.

## Current recommendation

The strongest new-name trio is **Graphmetry**, **CrawlFoundry** and **SignalMetra**. Across the full eleven-name list, **Indexiva** remains the strongest sounding option, while **Graphmetry** currently has the better balance of meaning, product breadth and search ownability.
