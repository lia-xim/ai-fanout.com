# Rebrand Top 20 — manual SERP review

Checked: 2026-09-01

## Method

- DataForSEO Google organic live SERPs, desktop, top 10, Germany (`2276/de`) and United States (`2840/en`).
- Every finalist was checked as an ordinary unquoted query; the strongest and ambiguous candidates were also checked as quoted exact queries.
- The final `.com` strings were checked against the official Verisign RDAP endpoint. Every one returned HTTP 404 at the final check. This is a point-in-time registration signal, not a reservation.
- Rankings below are manual judgements. Result counts were not used as an automatic score.
- This is search-collision research, not legal trademark clearance. EUIPO, DPMA, USPTO, company-register, social-handle and linguistic checks still belong in the final clearance of the last 2–3 names.

Additional DataForSEO provider cost in the final research sequence: approximately USD 2.53, below the authorised EUR 10 ceiling. No domain was purchased.

## Recommendation

Take **Zivunor**, **Indexmetry** and **Traceviq** into formal clearance. My preferred architecture is:

- `Zivunor — On-demand SEO Suite` for maximum long-term brand ownership; or
- `Indexmetry — SEO intelligence on demand` for faster product comprehension.

Do not put “SEO” inside the invented master brand. Put it in the descriptor, title tag, homepage H1, organisation schema and launch messaging. That gives Google an unambiguous category association without turning the company name into a generic keyword compound.
